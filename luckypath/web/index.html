ABOUT LUCKYPATH

How it works

Choose a destination and a time budget. Five preferences help select a route and nearby places.

Openness
A model of distance from buildings, compared with a target value. A higher priority does not always mean the widest street.
Greenery & quiet
Tree records from 2015 and distance from major roads. Current shade and noise are not directly measured.
Estimated time
Walking distance and network connections at your chosen pace, plus stops and buffer time. Actual travel time may differ.
Nearby places
Recorded places near the route, ranked by surrounding environments and distance. Amenities do not indicate wheelchair accessibility.
Saju & astrology
Optional analyses each suggest experience weights, averaged 50:50 for one route. These interpretations and their equal weighting are experimental, not validated predictions. Unknown birth time or location limits the available analysis.
Your details
Birth inputs are not saved in server files or route downloads. Save details explicitly to remember them in this browser; you can delete them from your profile.
